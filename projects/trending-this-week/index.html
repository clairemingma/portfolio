Trending This Week

[Client]

Phia

[Role]

Product Marketing Intern

[Discipline]

Data Analytics & Content Creation

[Year]

2025

Shopping is inherently social. People want to know what everyone else is buying, and Phia was sitting on hundreds of thousands of weekly data points, unused.

I began querying our shopper data to see what was actually there. Which products were being viewed, which brands, how that shifted week to week. Then I kept pulling the thread: did top products differ by city, was the fastest-growing item the same as the most-viewed one, what was the top product within a single brand or category.

The top trending products and brands post has run weekly since and continues today, roughly 60 posts to date. It became the seed of a larger product. We used it to start giving brands real insight into their shoppers, like what else the people who shop you also shop.

Alongside the trending series I ran the account more broadly. Over three months, I created 27 posts, carousels, and reels, pushing follower growth up 33.8%, about 35,000 new followers, with 6.6 million plus in total reach. The top single piece hit over a million views, 16,000 interactions, and 2,000 saves, roughly 14 times what typical content on the account was pulling.
