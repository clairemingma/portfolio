For Studio Edit 02, I sourced more than 25 creators across paid partnerships, product seeding, and invitations to the Silver Springs activation. With Pilates at the center of the drop, we needed creators with a genuine practice, which meant looking past follower counts for people who felt both aspirational and as trustworthy as a friend. I vetted each one against three content pillars (narrative, performance, and system of dress) so every creator could tell the product’s story, show it credibly in motion, or demonstrate how to style it. That shared standard kept the drop consistent across every channel, from paid social to the in-person activation.
