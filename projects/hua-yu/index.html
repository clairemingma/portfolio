A Chinese American wedding celebrates two loves: the love between two people, and the love of the families who raised them. As 2.1 million U.S.-born Chinese Americans enter their wedding years, a bride still finds herself choosing between honoring her family’s traditions and expressing her own style. Huā Yǔ, a ten-look Vera Wang Haute collection told through the symbolism of Chinese flowers, lets her do both, so she can feel like herself on the day she is most loved.
