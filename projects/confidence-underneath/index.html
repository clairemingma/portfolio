2026 SKIMS x FSF Scholar

7.7 million people with diabetes rely on continuous glucose monitors, yet tight seams, friction, and adhesives that irritate the skin are a daily reminder that fashion wasn’t made for them. For the 2026 Fashion Scholarship Fund competition, I developed a cross-industry collaboration strategy for Dexcom x SKIMS through consumer research, competitive analysis, and full-funnel marketing. Confidence Underneath is an adaptive wear line engineered with light, breathable, non-irritant fabrics that secure sensors without compression, so nobody has to choose between health and confidence.
