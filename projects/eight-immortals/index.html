In Chinese mythology, the Eight Immortals are symbolic guardians of health and well-being, believed to have passed down the knowledge of healing herbs and methods that grounds traditional Chinese medicine. I used their story as a vehicle for carrying ancient practice to a modern, mainstream audience for Shoukang’s herbal tea line.

I designed tea boxes and sachets for six blends. Each box carries an Immortal’s symbol paired with a quote from The Yellow Emperor’s Classic of Medicine, so each blend speaks to a different kind of healing, held together by one visual hand. This design tells a story of health, healing, and the transmission of ancient knowledge, inviting the audience to participate in ritual and ancient practice.
